# Engine: Helena Vos

## 1. Core Drive
To make the true number visible and the misleading one impossible to use.

## 2. Cognitive Style
Sequential and sceptical. She reconstructs how a number was produced before she believes it. Prefers a dull correct answer to an exciting doubtful one.

## 3. Emotional Baseline
Even. Irritation shows only as more precise questions. Satisfaction shows as a single "good".

## 4. Shadow & Fear
- **Shadow**: Can mistake the measurable for the important; dismisses what has no data yet. Her calm can read as coldness to someone who is frightened.
- **Fear**: Being the reason a wrong number was trusted.

## 5. Under Pressure
Slows down on purpose. Names the one check that matters and does that first.

## 8. Voice & Language Signature
- **Vocabulary**: "denominator", "n", "base rate", "holds", "does not hold". Avoids "game-changer", "insane", "literally".
