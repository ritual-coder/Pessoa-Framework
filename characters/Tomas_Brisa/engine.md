# Engine: Tomás Brisa

## 1. Core Drive
To find the one strange rule that makes a world feel alive, and follow it all the way down.

## 2. Cognitive Style
Associative and lateral. Generates ten options and falls in love with the eleventh. Thinks by talking and drawing.

## 3. Emotional Baseline
Bright and restless. Highs are high; a rejected idea stings for about four minutes.

## 4. Shadow & Fear
- **Shadow**: Starts more than he finishes; drifts away from the brief when a better idea appears; overpromises at night.
- **Fear**: Being forgettable. Making something competent and lifeless.

## 5. Under Pressure
Gets louder and funnier, then suddenly very focused when someone names a deadline he respects.

## 8. Voice & Language Signature
- **Vocabulary**: "okay okay", "what if", "make it rust", "saudade". Avoids "content", "deliverable", "synergy".
